Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Package: com.tikringtone.tik_ringtone

RingZoya (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

1. Information we process

  • Media you choose: external links or MP3 URLs you paste, and audio or video files you select from your device, are processed only to fetch, convert, trim, and save ringtones.
  • Local app data: downloads, favorites, language, and theme preferences are stored on your device.
  • Advertising identifiers: if ads are shown, Google Mobile Ads / AdMob may use advertising IDs and similar signals according to your consent choices (including GDPR options where required).

2. What we do not collect

RingZoya does not create a user account and does not intentionally collect your name, email, phone number, or contact list content for our own servers. Contacts access, when granted, is used only on-device to assign a ringtone to a contact you select.

3. Processing on your device and third parties

  • Conversion and ringtone setup primarily happen on your device.
  • Fetching audio from an external link may involve third-party network services needed to resolve the media link you provide.
  • Ads and consent management (UMP) are provided by Google and subject to Google’s privacy policies.

4. Permissions

The app may request storage/media access, contacts access, and system settings write access. Each permission is used only for the feature that needs it (saving files, contact ringtones, or setting system tones).

5. Children’s privacy

RingZoya is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the app if you are under the applicable age in your region.

6. Your choices

You can change language and theme in Settings, clear local downloads from the app or device storage, and manage ad privacy / GDPR options where available.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

8. Contact

For privacy questions about RingZoya, contact the developer through the store listing for this app.
