Privacy Policy for Gold Scale

Welcome to Gold Scale. We are committed to protecting and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our services.

1. Information Collection and Use

Gold Scale is a financial and technical utility application.

  • Personal Data: The app does not require you to provide sensitive personal identification information (such as your full name or national ID). The app may ask you to select your user profile/role (Trader, Goldsmith, or Investor) solely to customize your default interface and improve your user experience.
  • Local Data: The app may save certain settings locally on your device (such as your preferred default calculator, currency, or karat preferences) to enable quick access and offline functionality.

2. Advertisements and Third-Party Services

This application contains advertisements served by third-party ad networks (such as Google AdMob or similar advertising platforms) to help fund and provide the application free of charge to our users.

  • These advertising networks may collect and use anonymous data and device advertising identifiers (such as AdID or IDFA) to deliver personalized ads that are relevant to you.
  • We do not share any financial calculations, custom prices, or data you input into the calculators with these advertising networks.

3. Live Market Prices and Internet Connectivity

The application connects to the internet to fetch real-time live gold prices from global and local bullion market feeds. This connectivity is strictly used to update pricing data and does not upload or track any individual buying, selling, or conversion calculations performed within the app.

4. Data Security

We value your trust in using our app and care deeply about the security of your locally stored preferences. We implement industry-standard technical measures to protect it. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect updates in the application or app store regulatory compliance. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, please feel free to contact us via the support email provided on our app store listing page.
