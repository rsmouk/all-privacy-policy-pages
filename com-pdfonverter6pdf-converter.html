Privacy Policy

For the Android app PDF to PNG and JPG
Last updated: October 30, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how PDF to PNG and JPG (“the App”, package ID com.pdfonverter.pdf_converter) handles information when you use the App on Android.

The App converts PDF pages into PNG or JPG images on your device. We designed the App so that your documents stay on your phone and are not uploaded to our servers.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not operate a backend for this App that receives or stores:

  • Your PDF files
  • Converted images
  • Document contents or page text
  • Names, email addresses, or account credentials for App features

Conversion happens locally on your device. Selected PDF pages and resulting images are processed on-device and are not sent to us.

3. Information stored on your device

The App may store the following locally using device storage:

  • App preferences such as language, theme, and output quality
  • Temporary files needed to complete a conversion
  • Converted images you choose to keep or save to your gallery

These items remain under your control on the device. Uninstalling the App removes App-specific preference data. Images you saved to the system gallery remain until you delete them.

4. Permissions

Depending on your Android version and choices, the App may use:

  • Internet — to load advertisements
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID) — used by Google advertising services
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — optional alerts when a conversion finishes

The App does not request broad storage, media library, or camera access. Saving images uses the system gallery APIs without requiring full storage permission.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App may display ads through Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect and process device information related to advertising, such as advertising identifiers, IP address, device and app data, and interaction with ads.

Where required, we present a consent and privacy options flow for personalized ads. You can manage related choices in the App’s privacy options (when available) and in your device advertising settings.

For more detail, see Google’s policies: Google Privacy Policy and AdMob & privacy.

6. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the App may send local notifications only to inform you that a conversion has finished. These notifications are generated on your device and are not used for marketing messages from us.

7. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell your personal information. Document conversion is local. Third-party advertising services (Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads) may process advertising-related data as described above and in their own policies.

If you use the share feature, content is shared through the system share sheet with the app or destination you select. That destination’s privacy policy then applies.

8. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this App.

9. Data retention and security

Because conversion and preferences are handled on-device, retention is largely determined by what you keep on your phone. Temporary conversion files are cleaned up as part of normal App operation.

No method of electronic storage is perfectly secure. Keep your device locked and updated, and avoid converting sensitive documents on shared or untrusted devices.

10. Your choices

  • Deny or revoke notification permission in Android settings
  • Limit ad personalization in Android advertising / privacy settings
  • Use in-App privacy options for ads where the consent form is available
  • Delete converted images from the gallery or uninstall the App

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.
