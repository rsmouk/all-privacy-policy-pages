Privacy Policy

Magnifier Pro Max · Package: com.hesdev.magnifier_plus · Last updated: September 19, 2026

Magnifier Pro Max (“the App”), developed by hesdev (the data controller), helps you magnify objects with your device camera. This policy explains how personal data is handled under the GDPR and similar laws.

1. Controller & contact

Controller: hesdev
Website: https://hesdev.com
Privacy requests: https://hesdev.com/contact

2. Data we process

  • Camera preview: processed on-device only for live magnification. Not uploaded to hesdev servers.
  • Captured photos: stored locally in your gallery or app storage. Not uploaded to hesdev servers.
  • OCR text (if used): processed on-device via Google ML Kit. Images are not sent to hesdev servers.
  • App preferences: language and tutorial flags stored locally (SharedPreferences).
  • Advertising data (AdMob): Google may process device identifiers, approximate IP address, ad interaction data, and consent status under Google’s policies.

We do not create user accounts and we do not sell your personal data.

3. Purposes & legal bases (GDPR)

  • Core magnifier features (camera, local save, OCR): contract performance and/or legitimate interests to provide the service you requested (Art. 6(1)(b) and/or 6(1)(f)).
  • Personalized ads / ad measurement: consent via Google UMP where required (Art. 6(1)(a)). You may withdraw consent anytime in Ad Consent Settings.
  • Non-personalized ads (where allowed): as permitted by applicable advertising rules and your UMP choices.

4. Ads & UMP consent

We use Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). In the EEA, UK, Switzerland, and similar regions, a consent form is shown when required before ad requests. Ad requests are only made after UMP indicates that ads may be requested.

Manage or withdraw consent: Menu → About → Ad Consent Settings.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Retention

  • Photos and preferences remain on your device until you delete them or clear app data.
  • Ad-related data is retained by Google according to AdMob policies.
  • hesdev does not retain copies of your photos or camera stream on its own servers.

6. Your GDPR rights

Where GDPR (or UK GDPR) applies, you typically have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Rectify inaccurate data
  • Erase data (“right to be forgotten”), including deleting app photos and clearing app data in system settings
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Data portability where applicable
  • Withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior lawful processing
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority in your country

Contact us at hesdev.com/contact for controller-held data. For on-device data only: delete photos from the gallery or clear the App’s storage in system settings.

7. International transfers

When AdMob is used, Google may process technical data outside your country, including the United States, under Google’s standard contractual clauses and approved transfer mechanisms.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 16 (or the applicable local minimum age). We do not knowingly collect children’s data. If you are a parent and believe a child provided data via support channels, contact us to request deletion.

9. Permissions

  • Camera — live magnification
  • Photos / Gallery — save and view captured images
  • Internet — loading ads and related advertising services

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top reflects the current version. Continued use of the App after an update means you have reviewed the current policy.
