Privacy Policy

App: AR Draw
Package name: com.hesdev.ar_draw
Developer: hesdev (hesdev.com)
Last updated: September 4, 2026

AR Draw ("we", "our", or "the app") is a drawing and image-tracing application that helps users overlay images on a live camera view to draw on paper. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app may access, how it is used, and your choices.

Summary: AR Draw does not upload your photos or drawings to our servers. Camera and gallery access are used only on your device to display images for tracing. We do not sell your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

AR Draw is designed to work primarily on your device. We collect minimal information:

  • App preferences — Settings such as theme (light/dark), language, grid layout, favorites, and onboarding status are stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences.
  • Images you choose — Photos selected from your gallery or captured with the camera are processed locally on your device only. They are not transmitted to or stored on our servers.
  • Usage analytics (optional) — If enabled, Firebase Analytics may collect anonymized usage data (such as app opens and feature usage) to help us improve the app. This data does not include your photos or drawings.
  • Device tokens for notifications (optional) — If you enable push notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging may store a device token to deliver updates and tips. You can disable notifications at any time in app settings.
  • Advertising data (if ads are enabled) — If in-app ads are displayed through Google AdMob, Google may collect advertising identifiers and related data according to its own privacy policy.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Provide core app features (camera overlay, image tracing, favorites, and settings)
  • Remember your preferences on your device
  • Send optional push notifications about updates and tips (only if you opt in)
  • Improve app performance and user experience through anonymized analytics
  • Display ads if the ads feature is enabled in the app

3. Camera and Gallery Permissions

AR Draw requests camera access to show a live camera preview with a semi-transparent image overlay so you can trace drawings on paper. The camera is used for display purposes only during tracing sessions. We do not record, save, or upload video from your camera.

To let you choose your own reference images, the app uses the system image picker. This allows you to select a photo without requesting broad storage permissions on modern Android and iOS versions.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Your images and drawings remain on your device unless you choose to share them using your device's share feature.
  • App settings and favorites are stored locally on your device.
  • We do not operate servers that store your personal photos or artwork.
  • We take reasonable measures to protect any data processed by third-party services we use.

5. Third-Party Services

AR Draw may use the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase — Analytics and Cloud Messaging (Firebase Privacy)
  • Google AdMob — In-app advertising, when enabled (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google Play Services — Required for Android distribution

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these providers for more details on how they handle data.

6. Children's Privacy

AR Draw is suitable for children and families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country) beyond what is necessary for the app to function on the device. We do not require account registration. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will assist you.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share limited, anonymized data with third-party service providers (such as Firebase and AdMob) solely to operate and improve the app, as described in this policy.

8. Your Choices and Rights

  • Camera — You can deny camera permission; tracing with the live camera will not be available, but you may still browse sample drawings.
  • Notifications — You can enable or disable notifications in the app settings or your device settings.
  • Ads — You can limit ad personalization through your device's Google account settings.
  • Delete local data — Uninstalling the app removes locally stored preferences and favorites from your device.

Depending on your location, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy laws (such as GDPR). To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

9. International Users

AR Draw is available internationally. Third-party services we use may process data in countries other than your own. By using the app, you acknowledge that such processing may occur in accordance with the policies of those service providers.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
